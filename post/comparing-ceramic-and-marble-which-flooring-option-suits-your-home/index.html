Ceramic or marble: which flooring suits your home?

Two beautiful materials, with different personalities and care needs.

Consider the character you want

Ceramic tile offers a wide range of colors and patterns. Marble has natural veining and variation, so no two pieces look exactly alike. View several samples before deciding how much variation you enjoy.

Think about everyday care

Marble requires care suited to natural stone and can be affected by acidic spills. Ceramic is often a practical choice for busy spaces, although grout and surface texture still influence cleaning. Follow the product’s care instructions.

Match the product to the room

A tile that looks right is not automatically suitable for every floor. Ask about the intended use, wet-area suitability, and maintenance needs of the specific product you are considering.

Look beyond the tile price

Preparation, layout, edge details, and installation all shape the finished project. Compare complete proposals so you can choose based on the whole room, rather than the material cost alone.
